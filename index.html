What You Get

Everything to go from idea to post

ShareFuze puts 14 production tools into one app. Find the story, make the graphic, cut the clip, and queue it for every platform.

📊

Stat Hunter

Search, compare and rank NFL stats in seconds. The data comes from nflverse — the same source the analysts use.

🎨

Graphics Studio

Over 60 animated templates in both 16:9 and 9:16. Choose a template, drop in the stats, and export to OBS or a file.

🎬

Film Room

Split game film by play. The score-bug reader finds each play, ranks them by impact, and cuts the clips you pick.

🏈

Game Prep

Ten storylines for every game, powered by real data. Know the angles before kickoff — no guesswork.

📈

Team Report

About 40 measures for each team, ranked 1 to 32. See strengths, weaknesses and trends at a glance.

📦

Post Kit

Platform-sized files, captions in your voice, and a checklist for YouTube, TikTok, Shorts, Reels and X. You post — it prepares.

How It Works

From stat to screen in three steps

ShareFuze connects research, design and publishing into one flow. No copy-paste, no tab switching, no guesswork.

1

Find the Story

Search player and team stats, run comparisons, and let the Stat Hunter surface the take your audience wants to hear.

2

Build the Content

Pick a template, feed it the data, and the Graphics Studio renders an animated graphic or a video overlay — ready for OBS or export.

3

Queue for Every Platform

The Post Kit packages your files for YouTube, TikTok, Shorts, Reels and X. It writes captions in your voice. You upload on your schedule.

✦

You Stay in Control

ShareFuze never posts for you. Never. You decide what goes live and when.
